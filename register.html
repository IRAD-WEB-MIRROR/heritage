State Register of Monuments of History and Culture

The Register lists the immovable monuments protected under the heritage ordinance of 1997. Each monument has a protection number (year of entry / serial number). Categories: WH World Heritage, N national importance, L local importance, P provisional protection (up to two years, while a site is studied).

Only part of the Register is published on this site. Full extracts are issued at the Service on written request (form HPS-1).
