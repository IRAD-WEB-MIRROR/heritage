Vacancies

Posts in the Service are filled by open competition under the civil service rules of the Office of the Governor.

Open competitions

HPS-26/04

Inspector, Register department

Place of work
Talas, 14 Köhne Diz Street
Grade
Civil service, grade 8; from 3,300 som a month
Applications until
27 November 2026

Higher education in history, archaeology or architecture. Keeps Register files, prepares extracts and inspects monuments in Talas and the Talas District. Iradi and Russian; a driving licence is an advantage.

HPS-26/05

Conservator (brick, stone and terracotta)

Place of work
Talas and the Talas District, field work April to October
Grade
Civil service, grade 7; from 3,600 som a month, field allowance in season
Applications until
11 December 2026

Training in conservation or restoration and at least three years' work on historic buildings. Works at Rabat-i-Safed, the Mausoleum of Kenizek-Khatun and the monuments of the Old City.

HPS-26/06

Guide and attendant, Rabat-i-Safed open-air museum (seasonal)

Place of work
Rabat-i-Safed, 1 April to 31 October 2027
Grade
Contract; 2,700 som a month
Applications until
18 December 2026

Two posts. Secondary education; Iradi, Russian and good English. Leads tours, sells tickets, keeps visitors to the rules. Training in March.

How to apply

Bring or send to the Service (14 Köhne Diz Street, Talas, office 3) by the closing date: a letter of application naming the post and its reference, a curriculum vitae, copies of your identity document and diplomas, and the civil service personnel form (available at office 3).

Shortlisted candidates are invited to an interview within three weeks of the closing date. Enquiries: +996-39-3-22-41-17.
