Documents and legal acts

The acts under which the Service works and its own orders on permits, finds, works and visiting. Full texts of the Service's orders are given below; the heritage ordinance and its amendment are published in the official gazette.

Decision on the Register · 14 September 2026 · No. R-3/2026

Provisional protection: Kara-Suu I cave shelter and the Kara-Suu Canyon entrance rock art

In force

Enters the Kara-Suu I cave shelter as No. 26/101 and adds the canyon entrance panels to No. 13/062, both under provisional protection for two years; the excavation is closed to visitors and photography is not permitted.

Order of the Service · 12 April 2021 · No. 6

Works in the protection zone of the Old City of Talas

In force

Which repairs owners may do without approval, and what needs form HPS-3 with drawings; answer within 30 days.

Order of the Service · 22 April 2019 · No. 11

Filming and commercial photography at monuments (form HPS-7)

In force

A filming permit is needed for commercial photography and filming at registered monuments; at the Church of St. Janyl also the written consent of the archeparchy. Apply at least ten working days ahead; drones need a separate permit.

Amendment · 16 November 2010

Amendment to the heritage ordinance (finds of church property)

In force

Any find of church property made before 1992 (chalices, crosses, censers, icons, bells, books) is state property until claimed and must be declared to the Service within ten days; the finder receives a quarter of its valuation. Prohibits searching old chapel sites with metal detectors.

Ordinance · 8 April 1997

Heritage ordinance (on the protection of monuments of history and culture)

In force

Sets up the Heritage Protection Service and the State Register of Monuments of History and Culture; provides for protection zones, excavation permits (art. 14) and the approval of works on monuments; makes damage to a registered monument, unauthorised digging and the removal of finds offences. As amended in 2010.
