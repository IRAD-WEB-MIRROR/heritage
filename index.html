Welcome to the Heritage Protection Service

The Heritage Protection Service keeps the State Register of Monuments of History and Culture under the heritage ordinance of 1997, protects the monuments entered in it, runs the open-air museums and issues permits for excavations and for works on monuments. It is the state body for the two World Heritage properties on the territory of Irad.

Photography at monumentsPhotography for private use is allowed at most monuments. It is not permitted inside the Church of St. Janyl during services, in the cathedral library and archives, in the painted rock shelter, at excavations, in the occupied ravine cells and anywhere in the restricted zone of the Qara-Chik line. Each entry in the Register shows the rule for that monument. Commercial filming needs a filming permit.
