22 June 2016

Korgon of the Abdrai captains becomes a monument of national importance

At today's session on the State Register the Korgon of the Abdrai captains (Reg. No. 98/016), entered in 1998 as a monument of local importance, was raised to national importance. The Abdrai Heritage Foundation has completed the restoration of the gate tower, carried out from 2011 under the Service's approval.

The terminal of Kobozev Airport in Nurzhan (1968) was entered as a monument of local importance (Reg. No. 16/071). The building is fenced and unsafe; entry is prohibited.
