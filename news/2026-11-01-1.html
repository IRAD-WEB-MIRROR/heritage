1 November 2026

Thirty further entries of the Register published online

Thirty further entries of the State Register are now on this site: monuments of national and local importance in Talas, Nurzhan, Iyık Janyl and the districts, among them the Juma Mosque and the Church of St. Yuhanna in Talas, the Korgon of the Abdrai captains and the Mausoleum of Aulie-Ata. 45 entries are now published online; full extracts are issued at the Service on written request (form HPS-1).
