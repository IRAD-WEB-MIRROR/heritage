Leadership

The Heritage Protection Service is part of the Office of the Governor. It is headed by a Director, who has a first deputy and a deputy director; the open-air museums and the Iyık Janyl office report to the Director.

Navruz Khudoiberdiev

Navruz Khudoiberdiev

Director of the Heritage Protection Service · since 2010

Archaeologist, Talas State University. Led the works at the Caravanserai of Rabat-i-Safed for the 2014 Silk Roads listing.

The Director chairs the sessions on entries in the State Register, signs excavation permits and the Service's orders, and represents the Service in the World Heritage management plan for the Church of St. Janyl.

Structure

Register department

Keeps the State Register, prepares entries and changes of category, issues extracts (form HPS-1) and protection-zone certificates.

Tel. +996-39-3-22-41-21

Works and protection zones department

Approves repairs and building on monuments and in protection zones (form HPS-3) and inspects the works.

Tel. +996-39-3-22-41-22

Archaeology and finds department

Excavation permits (form HPS-2), season reports, declared finds (form HPS-5) and the valuation of finds.

Tel. +996-39-3-22-41-18

Open-air museums

The Caravanserai of Rabat-i-Safed: tickets, guides, school groups and upkeep of the site.

Tel. +996-39-3-22-41-30

Iyık Janyl office

World Heritage site management on Cathedral Hill and the Ruins of Old Iyık Janyl, together with the archeparchy; monuments of the Lower Naryn.

Tel. +996-39-6-22-37-40
